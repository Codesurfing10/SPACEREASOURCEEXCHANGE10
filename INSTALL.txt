SPACEREASOURCEEXCHANGE — Mining game bridge
==========================================
1. Copy:
   app/routes/mining_bridge.py  →  your repo app/routes/mining_bridge.py

2. Follow REGISTER.md to include the router + CORS in app/main.py

3. Optional env:
   MINING_GAME_API_KEY=...
   MINING_GAME_ORIGIN=...

4. Endpoints under /api/mining/* (prices, deposit, account, sell-mark)
